# Business case

Your prototype shows *what* the product looks like. This document explains *why* it could become a business. Answer in your own words and refer to course concepts by name. See the term project brief on Moodle for length and grading.

## 1. The idea

This is for mainly university students who are either beginners or who have quit before. Their problem is that they dont have any guidance, they find it hard to stay motivated, they find trainers too expensive and they dont have enough time. Right now people use lots of A.I meal tracking, youtube videos etc... But nothing tht combines everything. What Newrep does better is give you a full plan, acurate A.I meal tracking and cheap trainer check ins all in one place while the trainer holds you accountable. For the money aspect, it will cost $19 per check in, with 80% going to the trainer and 20% to Newrep. Then there will also be a premium version for meal tracking for an extra $5 monthly.

## 2. Where IT creates value

Before: finding a trainer means searching around and booking stuff in person and trainers would. have to find their own clients. 
With Newrep: Clients will come from the platform. The thing that makes this better is that trainers dont have to pay for ads and students can easily compare all trainers online by their specialty price and reviews all in one place.

Before: Trainer meetups and session happen at the gym for a decent period of time and takes time for both trainers and students to drive to the gym.
With Newrep: A 15 minute video call saves all the time from trevel to the gym and the gym time.

The A.I meal log also means the trainer dosnt have to spend time having meetings with you talking about what youve had to eat everyday, so the 15 minutes is spent strictly on talking about advice.

## 3. Network effects

Does your product get more valuable as more people use it?

When more trainers join the platform, students will have more options, shorter wait times and lower prices.
When more students join, trainers schedules fill up more and they get more earnings without having to find clients on their own.
This is a network effect because each new user makes the platform more valuable for the other side (trainers).
We will launch at one campus to start off and will recruit kinesiology students to become trainers and use the free plan to attract students. This will fill up both sides.

## 4. Can it be copied? (the moat question)

Anyone in this class could rebuild your website in a weekend with the same AI tool. So the code is **not** your competitive advantage.

I built the Newrep website in a few days with no coding experience so a profesional coder and website builder would probably do better than me. Which means the website is not to our advantage.

Rare and hard to imitate: a new app starts with no reviews and you cant build that overnight.

Switching cost: a trainer with 100 good reviews wouldnt leave because theyd have to restart their reputation.

Every meal log and check in teaches Newrep which plans actually work for students. After a year there will be lots of data that a brand new app would not have a could not buy. It gets better the more students that use it.

A student and a trainer could text each other directly to avoid the 20% fee. Trainers could also post on other sites. Also a big fitness app with millions of users could add the trainer check ins feature which would be hard to compete with.

My moat overall is medium. The website is easy to copy but reviews, trainers and data is what takes time to build.
